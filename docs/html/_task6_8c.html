Converts an NNF formula to Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF).

Main function to convert an NNF formula to CNF.

Recursively applies the distribution of OR over AND (using DISTR) to ensure the final formula is a conjunction of disjunctions of literals. This is the main recursive function for the CNF transformation step.

◆ CNF_FORMULA()

TreeNode * CNF_FORMULA ( TreeNode * phi)

Driver function for converting an arbitrary formula to Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF).

Driver function to execute the full CNF conversion process.

This function performs the standard three-step conversion process:

  1. Eliminate implications (IMPL_FREE).
  2. Convert to Negation Normal Form (NNF).
  3. Distribute OR over AND to achieve CNF (CNF). Prints intermediate results and frees intermediate trees.
Parameters
phiThe root of the initial formula tree.
Returns
TreeNode* The root of the final formula tree in CNF. The caller is responsible for freeing this tree.

TreeNode * NNF ( TreeNode * phi)

Converts a formula to Negation Normal Form (NNF).

Converts an implication-free formula to Negation Normal Form (NNF).

Applies rules for Double Negation ($\sim\sim A \equiv A$) and De Morgan's Laws ($\sim(A \land B) \equiv \sim A \lor \sim B$, $\sim(A \lor B) \equiv \sim A \land \sim B$) recursively until negations only appear immediately before atoms (literals). The input formula must be implication-free.
